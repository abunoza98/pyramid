    Privacy

    Privacy policy

    Last updated 23 October 2026

    The short version. Football Pyramids doesn't ask for your name, email address or any other details, and there are no accounts. Our web host handles the technical details every website sees. Ads on the site come from Google, which uses cookies, and you can say no to personalised ads.

    Who runs this site

    Football Pyramids is run by Arrow Sport Pty Ltd, based in Australia. For any question about this policy or your data, email:

    hello@footballpyramids.com

    What we collect

    We don't collect personal information directly. There are no sign-ups, comments or forms on the site.

    Hosting

    The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address and basic details about your browser when you visit, so it can deliver pages and protect the site from attacks. We don't use these details to identify you. Cloudflare's privacy policy explains how it handles this data. Live scores load from a separate service of ours, also run on Cloudflare (on its workers.dev address).

    What's stored in your browser

    If you use the club finder, the site remembers the club you picked in your browser's local storage, so it's highlighted on your next visit. This stays on your device and is never sent to us. To remove it, clear the club finder with the × button or clear your browser's data for this site.

    On fixtures pages, if you switch between times in your own time zone and local kick-off times, that choice is stored the same way.

    Advertising

    Football Pyramids is paid for by advertising, shown through Google AdSense.

    • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this site and other websites.
    • Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners show you ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
    • You can turn off personalised advertising in Google's Ad Settings. You can also opt out of other vendors' advertising cookies at aboutads.info or, in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu.

    Google explains more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

    Your choices in the UK, EU and Switzerland

    If you visit from the UK, the EU or Switzerland, you'll see a message asking for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can accept or refuse, and change your choice at any time with the Privacy and cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. If you refuse, you may still see ads, but they won't be based on your browsing.

    Analytics

    We don't run any analytics or tracking scripts of our own. If that changes, we'll update this page first.

    Fonts

    The site's fonts are stored on the site itself, so loading a page doesn't contact any outside font service.

    Children

    The site is for a general audience and isn't aimed at children under 16. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

    Your rights

    Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to how it's used. Because we don't collect personal information ourselves, most requests will concern Google or Cloudflare. You can contact them directly, or email us and we'll help where we can.

    If you're unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK, your local data protection authority in the EU, or the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

    Changes to this policy

    If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of the page.

    Football Pyramids is an independent site and is not affiliated with any league, federation or club.
