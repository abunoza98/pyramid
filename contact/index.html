About Football Pyramids

Each country's league system on one long page: the top division first, then every tier below it, with promotion, play-off and relegation places marked on each table.

What's covered

The home page shows the top divisions of England, Spain, Germany, France and Italy. Each country has its own page with the tiers below. More tiers and countries will be added over time.

Where the tables come from

The tables come from a football data provider and update through the day as results come in, usually within an hour of full time. Each table shows when it was last updated.
